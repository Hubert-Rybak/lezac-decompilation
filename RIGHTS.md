# Rights and provenance

The original **Larax & Zaco v1.0** game, its executable, assets, text, names, and
any derivative decompiler output are not claimed as original work of this
repository's contributors and are not relicensed here.

The analyzed release identifies Stefano Zanobi / Zanobi Software as its author.
Its bundled `ENGLISH.DOC` dates the release to April 23, 1996 and contains its
original distribution terms. Obtain and read those terms with your own copy.
This repository does **not** bundle the original ZIP, executable, or resource
files. `tools/fetch_original.py` imports the pinned archived shareware copy and
verifies all file hashes. Archival availability is not treated as public-domain
status or permission to relicense the game.

No blanket open-source license is asserted for recovered game logic or generated
pseudocode. REA and Ghidra are separately licensed upstream projects and are not
vendored. The repository is initially private.
